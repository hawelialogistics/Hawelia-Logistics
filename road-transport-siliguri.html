About Hawelia Logistics

A Siliguri-Based Transport Partner for Business Freight

Hawelia Logistics is a road transportation and logistics company based in Siliguri, West Bengal. We specialise in coordinating Full Truck Load transportation for businesses that need commercial and industrial goods moved by road.

Every movement has its own route, load capacity, destination and delivery requirements. Our focus is on coordinating suitable truckload transportation, maintaining responsive communication and supporting dependable execution from dispatch to destination.

Our transportation focus

We work with full truckload requirements, coordinating vehicles based on cargo details, route, destination and load capacity. Service suitability is confirmed against the specific consignment requirements.

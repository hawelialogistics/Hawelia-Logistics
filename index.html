01 / ABOUT US

Experience built
for the road.

Established in 1980, Hawelia Logistics is a road transportation and logistics management company headquartered in Siliguri, India. With over four decades of experience, we provide Full Truck Load (FTL) transportation solutions to support the movement of commercial and industrial cargo across India, with connectivity to Nepal and Bhutan.

Our operations are driven by a commitment to reliability, efficient coordination and disciplined execution. We serve businesses across sectors including cement, agriculture, mining and packaging, delivering transportation solutions aligned with their operational and supply chain requirements.

At Hawelia Logistics, we believe dependable transportation is fundamental to business continuity. We focus on building lasting business relationships through responsive communication, consistent service delivery and a professional approach to logistics management.
